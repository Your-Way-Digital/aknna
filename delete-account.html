Delete your account

Version 1.0 · Effective date: September 3, 2026

You can permanently delete your Aknna account and associated data at any time, either directly in the app or by email. This page explains both methods and what happens to your data afterward.

1 In the app

Open Aknna → Profile → Settings → Delete Account, then confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

2 By email

No access to the app? Email support@aknnaofficial.com from your account's registered address with the subject "Delete my account." We process these requests within 30 days.

What happens to your data

DELETED

  • Profile info (name, photo, email)
  • Wallet balance & transaction history
  • Chat messages & private photos
  • Gifts, points & referral data

RETAINED

  • Records legally required for tax/accounting purposes, kept for up to 5 years as described in our Privacy Policy

See our Privacy Policy for full details on data retention and your rights under GDPR, LGPD and CCPA/CPRA.
